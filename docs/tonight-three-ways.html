How to read the curve

Up the left is the Goobers’ chance of winning; left to right is the game clock. Green above the dashed even line, red below, as the app draws it.

The dotted grey line is the model that shipped before Tuesday — pre-game projection × time remaining, blind to the field. Where the lines sit together the rebuild changed nothing. Where they part is the argument.

Nine readings each; the live graph takes one every five minutes and will draw about forty tonight, into a week-long graph where tonight is a narrow band at the left.

The bug this fixed on Tuesday

σ used to be measured against expected finals — banked plus remaining. A big performance grew the denominator, shrank the spread, and made the model more confident because somebody had played well. Same remaining both sides: 10.7 banked gave σ 32.7, 60 banked gave 29.9. Backwards. It now scales on the two lineups’ full projected weeks, fixed once lineups lock, and reads 33.97 at kickoff in all three regardless.
